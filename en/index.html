01 — Our story

A place steeped in history.

Founded in 2002, La Sqala lies behind the ramparts of Casablanca's old medina, inside a fortified bastion. A Moorish café restaurant, open every day of the week.

Guests come for typical Moroccan cuisine made with fresh, high-quality produce, for the calm of an Andalusian garden, for a recital of oriental music.

Featured in every guidebook, La Sqala has become an essential meeting place for Casablancans and travellers seeking peace and serenity, in the heart of the historic medina.

Sqala, in the Sabir tongue

Sabir, spoken in the ports of North Africa by sailors, corsairs and merchants, blended words from every language of the Mediterranean.

03 — The Andalusian garden

In the heart of Casablanca, a garden where time stands still.

A sumptuous Andalusian garden sheltered by the ramparts: vines and palm trees, zellige fountains, mosaic tables in the shade. A place to enjoy the calm, a glass of tea, a recital of oriental music.

Breakfast in the morning, tea in the afternoon, dinner beneath the lanterns: the garden follows every moment of the day.

05 — The art of hospitality

Marhaban bikoum.

Welcome to Sqala.

« In the land of the setting sun, hospitality is no myth. It is with pleasure and warmth that we welcome you around a table, at La Sqala. »

The lantern and its shadows of light

  1. 01 / 05

    Tea

    Poured from high to raise a foam in the glass: Meknassi, Abdi, Soufi, Flio, Marrakchi. Tea is the first gesture of welcome.

  2. 02 / 05

    Hospitality

    Our “ambassadors”, who serve you in the garden, are attentive to every wish so that you feel at home.

  3. 03 / 05

    Craftsmanship

    Zellige tables, ceramics, chiselled lanterns, painted doors: Moroccan craftsmanship lives in every space.

  4. 04 / 05

    Architecture

    The ramparts of the old medina, the tower, the arches and the blue doors set the scene of the bastion.

  5. 05 / 05

    Ambience

    A place of life and cultural events, where you can attend a recital of oriental music.
